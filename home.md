# Spanish A1: First Steps

A beginner Spanish course for adults with no prior Spanish. Four lessons take you from greetings to ordering at a café, at the CEFR **A1** level. Each lesson takes about 45 minutes.

## How to use this course

1. Work through the lessons in order; each builds on the last.
2. Say every example out loud. Spanish spelling is close to how it sounds.
3. Try the Practice questions before opening the Answer key.

## Course outline

| # | Lesson | You will be able to |
|---|--------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Greetings and introductions](lessons/01-greetings.md) | Greet someone, give your name and ask theirs |
| 2 | [Numbers 0–20 and age](lessons/02-numbers.md) | Count to 20 and say how old you are |
| 3 | [Regular -ar verbs](lessons/03-ar-verbs.md) | Describe what you do with present-tense -ar verbs |
| 4 | [At the café](lessons/04-cafe.md) | Order food and drink and ask for the bill |

## Pronunciation in one minute

| Letter | Sounds like | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| a / e / i / o / u | ah / eh / ee / oh / oo, always short | *mesa* (MEH-sah) |
| h | silent | *hola* (OH-lah) |
| j | a breathy h, as in *house* | *jugo* (HOO-goh) |
| ll | y, as in *yes* | *me llamo* (meh YAH-moh) |
| ñ | ny, as in *canyon* | *año* (AH-nyoh) |
