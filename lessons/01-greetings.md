# 1. Greetings and introductions

## Goals

By the end of this lesson you can:

- greet someone at different times of day
- say your name and ask someone theirs
- say goodbye

## Greetings

| Spanish | English | When |
|---------|---------|------|
| Hola | Hello / Hi | Any time |
| Buenos días | Good morning | Until about noon |
| Buenas tardes | Good afternoon | Noon until dark |
| Buenas noches | Good evening / Good night | After dark, and when leaving at night |
| ¿Qué tal? | How's it going? | Informal |
| ¿Cómo está usted? | How are you? | Formal |
| ¿Cómo estás? | How are you? | Informal |

Spanish questions and exclamations open with an upside-down mark: **¿** and **¡**.

## Saying your name

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Me llamo Ana. | My name is Ana. (literally "I call myself Ana") |
| ¿Cómo te llamas? | What's your name? (informal) |
| ¿Cómo se llama usted? | What's your name? (formal) |
| Mucho gusto. | Nice to meet you. |
| Igualmente. | Likewise. |

## Saying goodbye

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Adiós | Goodbye |
| Hasta luego | See you later |
| Hasta mañana | See you tomorrow |

## Tú or usted?

Use **tú** with friends, family, children and people your age in relaxed settings. Use **usted** with older people, strangers in formal settings, and at work until invited to use *tú*.

## A short dialogue

> **Ana:** ¡Hola! Me llamo Ana. ¿Cómo te llamas?
>
> **Luis:** Hola, Ana. Me llamo Luis. Mucho gusto.
>
> **Ana:** Igualmente. ¿Qué tal?
>
> **Luis:** Bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?
>
> **Ana:** Muy bien. ¡Hasta luego!

## Practice

1. It's 9 a.m. Greet your new manager formally.
2. How do you say "My name is Sam"?
3. Someone says *Mucho gusto*. What do you reply?
4. Translate: "See you tomorrow."
