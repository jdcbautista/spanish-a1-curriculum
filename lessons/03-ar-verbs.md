# 3. Regular -ar verbs

## Goals

By the end of this lesson you can:

- conjugate any regular -ar verb in the present tense
- say what you and others do every day

## The pattern

Remove **-ar** from the infinitive and add the ending for the person. Here is **hablar** (to speak):

| Person | Ending | hablar |
|--------|--------|--------|
| yo (I) | -o | hablo |
| tú (you, informal) | -as | hablas |
| él / ella / usted (he / she / you formal) | -a | habla |
| nosotros / nosotras (we) | -amos | hablamos |
| vosotros / vosotras (you all, Spain) | -áis | habláis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes (they / you all) | -an | hablan |

The ending already shows who is acting, so the pronoun is usually dropped: *Hablo español* = I speak Spanish.

## Useful -ar verbs

| Verb | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| trabajar | to work |
| estudiar | to study |
| cocinar | to cook |
| escuchar | to listen to |
| tomar | to take / to drink |
| caminar | to walk |

## Saying "not"

Put **no** before the verb: *No trabajo los domingos* (I don't work on Sundays).

## Practice

Fill in the correct form.

1. Yo ___ (estudiar) español.
2. Nosotros ___ (cocinar) la cena.
3. ¿Tú ___ (trabajar) hoy?
4. Ellas ___ (escuchar) música.
5. Translate: "She doesn't walk to work."

2. cocinamos
