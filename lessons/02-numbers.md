# 2. Numbers 0–20 and age

## Goals

By the end of this lesson you can:

- count from 0 to 20
- ask someone's age and give yours

## Numbers 0–20

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 cero | 1 uno | 2 dos | 3 tres |
| 4 cuatro | 5 cinco | 6 seis | 7 siete |
| 8 ocho | 9 nueve | 10 diez | 11 once |
| 12 doce | 13 trece | 14 catorce | 15 quince |
| 16 dieciséis | 17 diecisiete | 18 dieciocho | 19 diecinueve |
| 20 veinte | | | |

16–19 are built as *diez y seis* ("ten and six") written as one word: **dieci-** + the unit.

## Saying your age

Spanish uses the verb **tener** (to have) for age: you *have* years.

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| ¿Cuántos años tienes? | How old are you? (informal) |
| ¿Cuántos años tiene usted? | How old are you? (formal) |
| Tengo veinte años. | I am twenty (years old). |
| Mi hermano tiene doce años. | My brother is twelve. |

Before a masculine noun, **uno** becomes **un**: *Tengo un hermano* (I have one brother).
